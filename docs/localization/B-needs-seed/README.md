# B-needs-seed — tasks waiting on words

**Seven open: [B68](B68-the-family.md)–[B74](B74-partners-and-friends.md)**, the
[P11](../../features/P-planning/P11-family-and-relationships/README.md) kin terms, filed on
2026-09-22: one ticket per branch of the family, **41 concepts** to seed and **37 glosses** to
author, plus a re-point of PARENT's — 38 definitions in all. Read them in order — B68 seeds
CHILD_OFFSPRING, MOTHER and PARENT's new plurals, which the other six stand on — and note one engine defect B69's seed has to
fix: Spanish puts the personal *a* after *tener*. Four of the 41 stay on the English literal by
design (SON and DAUGHTER, which would close a circle with CHILD_OFFSPRING; MOM and DAD, which differ
from MOTHER and FATHER only in register), each named in its file with every lead probed. **None of
the seven waits on P11's own engine work**: a definition has no possessor, so no tooltip reads the
`possessed`, `honorific`, `kin` or `with_<ADJECTIVE>` columns that feature adds.

[B59](../done/B59-time-words.md)–[B67](../done/B67-place-and-focus-adverbs.md), the
[P09](../../features/P-planning/P09-core-vocabulary/README.md) core vocabulary, were filed on
2026-09-22 and authored the same day; all nine are in [`done/`](../done/), and so is every B ticket
before them. B01–B58 retired earlier.

The nine were the first B tickets whose concepts **did not exist at all**: the opening rows of each
**Seed first** table were the P09 words themselves, then the differentia their glosses needed, so
authoring one was P09's seeding and the tooltips in a single pass. Between them they **seeded 73
words and authored 56 glosses**, taking the corpus from 538 concepts to 611 and from 374 composed
definitions to 430. Six words two tickets shared (TAKE, HAND, POINT_NOUN, STILL, DAY, WEEK) were
seeded first on their own, because one ticket's gloss stood on another's word.

Eleven of their concepts are seeded and still wait for a gloss, each owned by one
[C ticket](../C-needs-engine/): DAY, WEEK and YEAR on numerals (C31); TODAY, JUST and STILL on a
temporal complement (C29); SHOULD and MIGHT on a content clause (C30); ONLY on an indefinite pronoun
(C32); THERE on the French distal demonstrative (C40); and the differentia ALLOWED beside LET (C36).
EVEN is the one word the batch could not seed at all (C39). Three more are literal by design (TRY,
AMERICAN and RIGHT_SIDE), and four differentia the batch bought are unglossed roots: MIND, ERROR and
REALITY of [C26](../done/C26-root-nouns-on-the-literal.md)'s kind, DIRECT_VERB of
[C28](../done/C28-verb-roots-without-a-gloss.md)'s. Each is named in the ticket that seeded it.

**A file belongs here** when the plan shape works but a concept it references is not seeded yet. Its
**Seed first** section lists the missing words with proposed forms in all seven languages —
suggestions for the seed author, **not renders** — and its **Unlocks** section gives the plan each
word turns on. Seed them (the [`seed`](../../../.claude/skills/seed/SKILL.md) skill) and the task
becomes an [A](../A-ready/), which
[`/localize-seed`](../../../.claude/skills/localize-seed/SKILL.md) or
[`/localize`](../../../.claude/skills/localize/SKILL.md) then authors.

Every file also carries a **Not solved by this seed** section naming the concepts the words would
*not* buy, and where they go instead. Read it before seeding: it is what keeps the ratio honest.
The rule the sweeps of 2026-09-22 applied is that **a word is seeded when a gloss in the batch uses
it** — seventeen of B52–B58's 57 proposed words were skipped on that ground, and
[B55](../done/B55-sequence-and-position.md) is the extreme case, proposing three words, needing none
of them, and shipping its one concept on vocabulary already in the corpus. B59–B67 skipped every word their own
**Not solved** sections deferred — among them GRASP, SOLVE, STUDY, TEACH, EARN, HOUR, MONTH, EARTH,
SUN, SLEEP, MOMENT, TRUE, DIFFERENT, SIDE, HEART, UNITED_STATES, EFFORT, CONTINUE and PERHAPS — each
named in its file with the proposed forms kept for whoever unblocks it.
