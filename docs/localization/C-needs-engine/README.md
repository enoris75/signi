# C-needs-engine — tasks blocked on a construct, or deferred

**Six open, 199 concepts — C23–C28**, all filed by the
[sweep of 2026-09-22](../localization-tasks.md#the-sweep-of-2026-09-22). The number is large and
mostly not a backlog: **76 of the 199 are primitives** — TIME, PLACE, CONCEPT, CREATE, CHANGE, BE —
the words every other gloss is built out of, and the right outcome for them is no plan. They are
enumerated in [C26](C26-root-nouns-on-the-literal.md) and
[C28](C28-verb-roots-without-a-gloss.md) so that a later sweep does not re-probe them.

**One construct dominates the rest.** A *headless relative clause* — the object-gap clause
`patientGloss` already builds, rendered without a head — would unblock
[C23](C23-participial-state-adjectives.md)'s 22, most of
[C24](C24-grammar-feature-adjectives.md)'s 53 and two of [C27](C27-grammar-meta-nouns.md)'s, plus
ten deferrals in [B54](../B-needs-seed/B54-sensation-and-quality-adjectives.md): about **eighty
concepts**, more than any other single piece of engine work this catalogue has named. A *part-whole
complement* is second; a *locative `MannerRelation`* ([C25](C25-place-and-direction-adverbs.md)) is
the cheapest.

A file belongs here when the string or gloss needs a grammatical construct the engine cannot render
yet — its **Blocked on** section names it, with a probe showing what comes out today — or when it is
deliberately left on the English literal because no distinguishing phrase can be composed
([C15](../done/C15-ui-literal-by-design.md) collects the UI ones, C26 and C28 the concepts). A C
task retires either by building the construct or by splitting into the A and B tasks its pieces turn
out to be.

C01–C22 are in [`done/`](../done/), including the three that were retired by splitting rather than
built (C05, C08, C18). See
[the index](../localization-tasks.md#part-c--needs-engine--deferred-c-needs-engine) for the
encoding.
