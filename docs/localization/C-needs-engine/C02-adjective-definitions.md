# C02. Adjective definitions — all 47 adjectives

**Blocked on:** an **adjective (predicate) render mode**. An adjective's definition is a predicate or
attributive phrase ("large in size", "feeling joy", "at a low temperature"), which no current plan
shape produces — a `PhrasePlan` builds a noun phrase or a clause, not a bare predicate.

## What's needed (grammatical construct)

A way to render an adjective gloss, e.g. a copular predicative fragment ("being large") or an
attributive noun phrase with a scalar/dimension complement ("large in size" = BIG + "in" + SIZE).
The latter also needs dimension nouns (SIZE, TEMPERATURE, QUALITY) seeded.

## Blocks (representative)

BIG ("large in size"), SMALL, HOT/COLD ("at a high/low temperature"), HAPPY ("feeling joy"),
STRONG, GOOD, … (47 total, minus the grammar-technical ones that may stay literal regardless).

Until the render mode lands, all adjectives stay on the English literal.
