# P18. Lithuanian — *lietuvių kalba* as a new output language

**Feature:** Lithuanian (`lt`) renders every phrase the other languages render, as a row in the
translations panel and, once reviewed, as an interface language.
**Shape:**
- **General groundwork:** already shipped (P10/P04/P03 §0): one language list, no database CHECK,
  `preview`/`ready` status, ready-only test gating, preview columns in `concepts/<code>/` with a
  borrowing chain in `concepts/columns.ts`. Lithuanian adds no general groundwork.
- **Case-and-aspect groundwork (§0):** shipped with [P05 Polish](../P05-polish/README.md) on
  2026-09-28: case keys in the lexicon, optional `pf_` aspect keys, the reflexive possessive through
  the plan's `coreferent: 'subject'`, relatives declined by case. Lithuanian takes Polish's keys
  ([style-pl.md](../P05-polish/style-pl.md)) unchanged wherever the two languages agree.
- **Column:** `packages/backend/src/concepts/lt/`, every concept, nothing borrowed.
- **Engine:** a new folder `packages/engine/src/languages/lt/`, forked from `pl` (D9).

**Status:** **E1–E11 shipped, 2026-09-28** — Lithuanian renders as a `preview` row: its own column
(all 846 concepts in every case and both aspects, none borrowed), an engine forked from `pl`, every UI
string (796) and definition (621). E12 (native review) and E13 (promotion) are open — see
[§6](#6-tasks-e1e13). D1–D12 were taken as recommended; D9 and D10 were revised after P05 shipped (fork
and borrow from `pl`).

## Why

- Lithuanian has about 3 million speakers and is an official language of the EU. It is one of the two
  living Baltic languages, and among the most conservative Indo-European languages alive.
- **It is the first language here with a locative case.** Location needs no preposition (*namuose*), so
  the complement layer has to let a case stand where every other engine puts an adposition.
- **The genitive precedes the head** (*katės maistas*, *semantinių frazių kūrėjas*), unlike Polish,
  Russian and Ukrainian: one more reason the case groundwork belongs to no single engine.
- **It is simpler than Polish where Polish is hardest:** no gendered past (*valgė* for everyone), no
  masculine-personal class, no neuter nouns, possessive pronouns that do not decline (*mano, tavo,
  savo*), and a verb that does not agree in number in the 3rd person, so quantifier government needs no
  new agreement rule.
- Whichever of P05/P06/P07/P18 goes first proves the case groundwork; Lithuanian proves it with the
  smallest engine.

## 0. Case-and-aspect groundwork — shipped with P05

P05 shipped §0 before P18 started; Lithuanian uses it as P05 built it, with these notes.

### 0.1 Aspect keys

P05 §0.1 unchanged: imperfective forms under today's keys, perfective under `pf_`. Lithuanian's
perfective future is synthetic like Polish's (*suvalgys*), so `pf_{p}_future` holds it; the imperfective
future is also synthetic (*valgys*), stored under `{p}_future`. No *być*-style periphrasis.

### 0.2 Case keys

P05 as shipped: `gen_sg, dat_sg, acc_sg, ins_sg, loc_sg, voc_sg` and `gen_pl … loc_pl`; nominative in
`base` and `plural`. Lithuanian has the same seven cases, so the key set fits as is (the vocative for
address, as Polish uses it). `gender` is `masc | fem` only: Lithuanian nouns have no neuter.

- **Adjectives** add one form nothing else stores: the **neuter** predicative (*gera, gražu, šalta*),
  used when the subject has no gender (an infinitive or clause subject: *valgyti gera*). Stored as
  `neuter`, derived by the helper.
- **Pronouns:** declined in `pronoun_forms` under the same keys.

### 0.3 The aspect-selection table

P05 §0.3, with Lithuanian's column:

| engine cell | aspect | lt |
|---|---|---|
| present, neutral | imperfective | valgo |
| past, neutral | perfective | suvalgė |
| future, neutral | perfective | suvalgys |
| progressive (any tense) | imperfective, simple tense | valgo / valgė / valgys |
| prospective | perfective future after *tuoj* | tuoj suvalgys |
| resultative | *būti* + perfective active past participle | yra suvalgiusi / buvo suvalgiusi |
| with a frequency adverb, present | imperfective | visada valgo |
| with a frequency adverb, **past** | imperfective **frequentative past** | visada valgydavo |
| under a modal | perfective infinitive; imperfective if the modal is negated | turi suvalgyti / neturi valgyti |
| conditional | perfective | suvalgytų |
| imperative | perfective; imperfective when negative | suvalgyk / nevalgyk |
| negation otherwise | unchanged aspect | nesuvalgė |

The frequentative past (*-davo*) is Lithuanian's one extra cell. It is regular from the infinitive stem,
so it costs one helper and one row of the table, not a new plan field.

### 0.4 Reflexive possessive

P05 found `subjectAntecedent` unnecessary: a possessor coreferent with the subject already reaches the
engine as `coreferent: 'subject'` (P11-E2). *savo* for it, *jo/jos/jų* otherwise. Lithuanian needs it for every person, not only the 3rd: *aš valgau **savo** maistą* is the
norm, *mano maistą* marked.

### 0.5 Relative pronouns

*kuris / kuri*, declined for the head's gender and number, case from its role (P05 §0.6;
`de/relativePronoun.ts` is the model). *Kas* after a pronoun head (*tas, kas …*).

### 0.6 A case where the others have an adposition

The locative complement today always renders an adposition. Lithuanian's plain "in" is the **locative
case alone** (*namuose*, *mieste*). The engine's complement builder must accept "no preposition, case
`loc`" as a realisation. `de` already renders a bare case in one place (the dative terminus), so the
shape exists; the new part is doing it for a locative.

## 1. Data — what a Lithuanian column costs

Measured from the Catalan column (846 concepts, all complete):

| role | concepts | authored | generated cells | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| noun | 440 | stem + class (+ irregulars) | ~5,700 | `base, plural, gender` + 10 case forms (D3) |
| verb | 205 | 3 principal parts, ×2 where a perfective is stored | ~11,000 | per aspect: infinitive; 6 × present, past, frequentative past, future, conditional; 3 × imperative; active past participle (4 forms, for the resultative); passive past participle (4 forms, A01); the half-participle *valgydamas* (process-level instrumental) |
| adjective | 153 | base (+ class where the ending is ambiguous) | ~460 | `base, fem, neuter`; declension by rule in the engine; comparative *-esnis*, superlative *-iausias* by rule (irregular: *geras → geresnis*, regular after all) |
| adverb | 40 | as is | ~50 | *niekada, niekur, niekas* carry `polarity: negative` |
| pronoun | 7 | full | ~100 | *aš, tu, jis/ji, mes, jūs, jie/jos*, each in 6 cases; *savęs* |

**About 2,100 principal parts in, ~17,500 cells out.** The helpers are the risk (a wrong class helper

Verbs to settle first, because the UI strings and definitions lean on them: *būti* (*esu, yra, nėra*),
*turėti, galėti, norėti, eiti, ateiti, daryti/padaryti, duoti, tapti, sakyti, matyti, žinoti*.

## 2. The engine — `packages/engine/src/languages/lt/`

One function per file with a colocated test, as every folder does. Expect **~55 source files,
~1,500 LOC**: fewer than Polish (no articles, no gendered past, no clitics) but more than Catalan
(declension in every noun-phrase word).

### 2.1 Noun phrase

- **No articles.** Definite, indefinite and bare all render nothing.
- **Case by slot:**

  | slot | case |
  |---|---|
  | subject | nominative |
  | direct object | accusative; **genitive when the verb is negated** (obligatory, unlike Polish/Russian's partial rule) |
  | terminus | dative |
  | possessor | genitive, **prenominal** (*katės maistas*) |
  | noun modifier | genitive, prenominal (*frazių kūrėjas*) |
  | predicative noun after *būti* | nominative (*yra legenda*) |
  | predicative noun after *tapti* | instrumental (*tampa legenda*) |
  | predicative adjective | nominative, agreeing; **neuter** with a genderless subject |
  | passive agent | genitive, no preposition (*katės suvalgyta*) |

- **Adjectives:** prenominal, declined by rule from `base` in three classes (*-as/-a*, *-us/-i*,
  *-is/-ė*), agreeing in gender, number and case. Plain forms only (D8).
- **Determiners:**

  | value | lt | effect |
  |---|---|---|
  | this / that | *šis, ši* / *tas, ta* | declines with the noun |
  | all | *visi, visos* | agrees |
  | no | *joks, jokia* | agrees **and** negates the verb (*jokia katė nevalgo*) |
  | some | *kelios / keli* | agrees (plural) |
  | many / few | *daug* / *mažai* | the noun goes **genitive plural**; the verb is 3rd person anyway, so nothing else changes |

- **Possessives:** *mano, tavo, jo, jos, mūsų, jūsų, jų, savo* — indeclinable, prenominal.
- **Degree:** synthetic comparative and superlative by rule; *labiau / labiausiai* for adjectives the
  rule does not fit (participial adjectives).

### 2.2 Verb group

- **Pro-drop:** D6.
- **Tense × aspect:** §0.3. No agreement in gender in any finite tense; the resultative's participle
  agrees in gender and number with the subject (*katė yra suvalgiusi*, *katinas yra suvalgęs*).
- **Negation:** *ne-* **written as one word** with the finite verb (*nevalgo*, *nesuvalgė*); the
  irregular negated *būti*: *nesu, nesi, **nėra**, nesame, nesate*. With a prefixed reflexive the
  *-si-* moves in after *ne-* (*prausiasi → nesiprausia*). Negative concord: *niekada ne-*, *niekas
  ne-*, *joks … ne-*.
- **Reflexive verbs:** the *-si* suffix (*praustis, prausiasi*), infixed after a prefix
  (*nusiprausti*). Stored whole in each cell, as Catalan stores *em torno*; the engine only moves *-si-*
  for negation.
- **Modals:** *turėti* (MUST), *galėti* (CAN), *norėti* (WILL/want), inner modals as infinitives
  (*nori galėti eiti*).
- **Copula:** *būti*; BECOME = *tapti* + instrumental. Existential *yra* / negated *nėra* + genitive
  (*nėra katės*, "there is no cat").
- **Generic subject:** D7.
- **Passive** (A01): *būti* + passive past participle agreeing with the subject, agent in the genitive.

### 2.3 Complements — case and preposition

| complement | Lithuanian | case |
|---|---|---|
| locative | bare **locative** (*namuose*); specifiers *ant* (on), *po* (under), *už* (behind), *prieš* (in front of), *prie* (near), *aplink* (around) | *ant, už, prie* + gen; *po* + ins; *prieš, aplink* + acc |
| direction | *į* (*į namus*); to a person *pas* (*pas vaiką*) | *į* + acc; *pas* + acc |
| source | *iš* (out of), *nuo* (away from: *nuo namų*) | genitive |
| route | *per* (*per parką*) | accusative |
| cause | *dėl* (because of, fault); *dėka* after the noun (thanks to: *draugo dėka*) | genitive |
| manner | similative *kaip* (*kaip vanduo*); mode *su* (*su džiaugsmu*); means: bare instrumental | *kaip* + nom; *su* + ins |
| instrumental | bare **instrumental** (*peiliu*); process level: half-participle (*rinkdamas žodį*) | instrumental |
| terminus | bare **dative** (*vaikui*) | dative |
| predicative | none | §2.1 |

*Dėka* is a **postposition**: the one place the complement builder puts the adposition after the noun.

**Coordination:** *ir*, *bet / tačiau*, *arba / ar*.

### 2.4 Moods

- **Conditional:** from the infinitive stem, stored per D4 (*valgyčiau, valgytum, valgytų, valgytume,
  valgytumėte*).
- **If-clause:** ***jei*** (or *jeigu*) + conditional; the main clause is conditional too.
- **Imperative:** *-k* forms (*valgyk, valgykime, valgykite*); perfective by default, imperfective when
  negated (§0.3).
- **Content clauses:** *kad* + indicative; *kad* + conditional after verbs of wanting (*noriu, kad
  valgytų*). A `CONTENT_CLAUSE_MOOD` entry in the translator tables.

## 3. Interface

- **Language names are genitive plurals of the people + *kalba*:** Lithuanian is *lietuvių kalba*, and
  in a menu just *lietuvių*. The other languages: *anglų, italų, prancūzų, vokiečių, ispanų, japonų,
  portugalų, šveicarų vokiečių, retoromanų, katalonų*. The LITHUANIAN concept stores *lietuvių kalba*
  with *kalba*'s case forms (*lietuvių kalbos, lietuvių kalbą*, …), so "in Lithuanian" renders
  *lietuvių kalba* (instrumental, which is spelt the same) correctly. The row label is therefore
  *Lietuvių kalba*; whether a menu should show the bare *Lietuvių* is a ruling for E12, and would be a
  label format, not a second concept.
- **Lithuanian in each language:** *Lithuanian, lituano, lituanien, Litauisch, lituano, lituano,
  リトアニア語* (reading *りとあにあご*), gsw *Litauisch*, rm *lituan* (verify per idiom), ca *lituà*.
- **Flag:** 🇱🇹 (D11).
- **Fonts:** Lora, Inter and Playfair Display cover *ą č ę ė į š ų ū ž* (Latin Extended-A); check
  Playfair's *ė* and *į* render, not fall back.
- **Typeahead:** search already folds diacritics (P03 §3); check *zuvis* finds *žuvis* and *ziema*
  finds *žiema*.
- **UI instruction register:** the infinitive (*Išsaugoti frazę*), as Lithuanian software writes it.

Built by seven worktree agents (three noun slices, two verb slices, adjectives/adverbs/pronouns, the
engine), merged into `p18-lithuanian`. The helper defects the column lanes met were fixed in
`helpers.ts` once every lane had landed (the *-tojas* locative *-juje*, *atvejo*, *dančiui*, *tuštesnis*,
the hard feminine participle *nešusi*, the 2sg *keiti*, the prefixed future *įgis*).

**Where the engine departs from this plan**, each `(verify)` in the code and a ruling owed in E12:

- The *jei* clause is imperfective (*jei šuo bėgtų*), as the opening table has it; §0.3's "conditional:
  perfective" holds for the main clause only.
- A content clause without a mood of its own is *kad* + conditional (`CONTENT_CLAUSE_MOOD`); a factive
  *gerai, kad* would want the indicative, and a wanting verb needs `content_clause_mood` in the column.
- The present passive is *yra valgyta* (the past participle): the present passive participle *valgoma*
  is not stored.
- The prospective past is *ruošėsi suvalgyti*; the frequentative is used only with ALWAYS, OFTEN,
  REPEATEDLY, SOMETIMES, USUALLY (NEVER keeps the simple past, *niekada nevalgė*).
- Direction to a person is *pas* + accusative; temporal *at* the bare accusative unless the noun names
  its preposition; the comparative standard *nei* + nominative; the existential puts the place first
  (*namuose yra katė*); a 3rd-person imperative is *tegul* + present.
- The negated *eiti* contracts (*neina, nėjo*).
- A generic subject is never said (D7): *valgo pelę*, *yra laimingas*.

## 7. Risks

- **Helpers multiply errors.** A wrong ending in a class helper is wrong for every noun in that class;
  the review sheet is grouped by class for that reason.
- **Accent classes are invisible but matter.** Standard spelling does not mark stress, but some forms
  depend on the accent paradigm (*-as* nouns whose locative plural is spelt the same whatever the stress
  are fine; a handful of *u*-stem and *i*-stem forms are not). Mark each such noun *(verify)*.
- **Aspect is a modelling choice** (P05 §6), milder here because many verbs have no stored perfective.
- **Snapshot growth.** The conjugation snapshot grows by two aspects and five tenses; give `lt` its own
  snapshot file at promotion, as P05 §6 suggests for the Slavic languages.
- **Language-name labels** (*lietuvių kalba* vs *lietuvių*) touch every language row's label; the rule
  must be a label format, not a second concept.

## Out of scope

Dialects and Samogitian; the vocative outside address; definite (pronominal) adjectives (D8); written stress marks;
numerals and their case government; the illative (*namop*) and other archaic local cases; the
*būdinys* (*bėgte bėgo*); participial clauses beyond the resultative, the passive and the process-level
half-participle; a completion flag on the verb phrase.
