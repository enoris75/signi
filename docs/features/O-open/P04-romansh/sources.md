# P04 — Romansh reference works, terms of use and reviewers

[P04 D11](README.md#decisions), [P04-E3 D1, D4](Z-done/P04-E3-sources-and-reviewers.md). One row per
reference work the three columns (E4–E6) may draw on, with its terms of use and a ruling:

- **copy** — the work's own terms allow forms to be copied into the corpus;
- **consult** — forms may be looked up to check a draft, one at a time, but the column is authored,
  not copied, and nothing is bulk-downloaded or scraped. **A work with no stated terms is *consult*.**
- **unavailable** — not reachable for this project.

Checked 2026-09-27 (web search and the publishers' pages). Nothing below is legal advice; a
*copy* ruling needs the licence text in hand, which no row has yet.
