# C6. Romance simple past is the perfective

**Looks wrong, is right. Do not "fix".**

Romance simple past is the perfective (`mangiò`, `mangea`), not the periphrastic perfect —
a deliberate mapping; the perfect is `aspect: 'resultative'`.

This holds for an event verb. A state verb (WILL, CAN, MUST, BE, HAVE, OWN …) takes the imperfect
in the past (`voleva`, not `volle`): that was a real defect, fixed in [A130](../fixed/A130-romance-past-of-a-state-verb.md).
